06 / GOOD QUESTIONS

A little clarity,
before we start.

Something else on your mind?
Just ask us.

Do I need to understand AI?+

No. Tell us about your business and what you need. We handle the technical work and show you how to use what we build.

How much does a project cost?+

It depends on the scope. We’ll discuss your budget, recommend a practical approach, and give you a clear price before work begins.

Will I be paying a monthly subscription?+

Our projects don’t require an agency subscription. Your solution may have separate costs for things like hosting or third-party software, which we’ll explain before you commit.

Can I update it myself?+

We aim to make routine updates manageable for you. We’ll agree on what you want to control and provide guidance, including AI-assisted options where they make sense. More complex changes may still need design or development help.

Can you work with the tools I already use?+

We’ll review your existing systems and recommend what can be connected, improved, or kept as it is.

What if I don’t know what I need?+

That’s a good place to start. Tell us what feels difficult, repetitive, or overdue, and we’ll help identify a useful next step.
